About Plastal-Bot Builders

Plastal-Bot Builders is a robotics and STEM education organisation based in Lusaka, Zambia. Our mission is to empower young people to become innovators and problem-solvers by giving them practical skills in robotics, programming and engineering.

Our mission

To empower young people to become innovators and change-makers by equipping them with the skills and knowledge to create sustainable solutions through technology, robotics and environmental advocacy.

Where we work

Plastal-Bot Builders is based in Lusaka, Zambia, and delivers programmes in schools and communities across the country.
